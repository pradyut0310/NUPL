Our role

Your construction-material partner in Odisha.

Niyati Udyog Pvt. Ltd. (NUPL) is an authorised distributor of JSW NeoSteel, JSW One TMT, and Finolex Pipes, Wires and Cables, serving the construction sector across Odisha. We partner with builders, contractors, infrastructure developers, government organisations, industrial clients and channel partners by supplying premium TMT bars, coated roofing sheets, structural steel and piping solutions.

As an authorised distributor of JSW NeoSteel in Odisha, NUPL is committed to making quality reinforcement steel and other essential construction materials accessible to the market.

Our product portfolio extends beyond steel to include cement, paints, colour-coated roofing sheets, pipes, wires and cables. By bringing multiple construction-material categories together, we aim to simplify sourcing requirements and provide customers with a dependable supply partner for different stages of construction.

Our approach is built around product quality, established brand associations, customer relationships and responsive business support.
